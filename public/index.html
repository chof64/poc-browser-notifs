Add to Home Screen (iPhone / iPad)

    
Apple provides no automatic install button — follow these steps in Safari. Takes ~20 seconds. This page cannot tap Safari's Share button for you.

    
You are in a Safari tab (not installed).


    

      
On iPhone — look at the bottom toolbar

      
↓

      
◀▶⎙ 📖▢

      
Tap the Share button in the bottom-center of Safari, then scroll down to Add to Home Screen.

    


    

      
On iPad — look at the top-right of Safari

      
◀▶🔍 address bar⎙ 

      
↑

      
Tap the Share button at the top of Safari, then Add to Home Screen.

    


    
    
      
  1. Open this page in Safari (not Chrome, not an in-app browser like Messenger / Facebook / Gmail).
    2. 
      
  2. Tap Safari's Share button  (see mockup above for where it is).
    3. 
      
  3. Scroll the share sheet down and tap Add to Home Screen → Add (rename if you like).
    4. 
      
  4. Open the new Push POC icon from your Home Screen — not from inside Safari.
    5. 
      
  5. Inside the installed app: Enable notifications → Subscribe → Send to this device. Then lock the screen to prove it arrives like a native push.

Requires iOS / iPadOS 16.4 or later. Check: Settings → General → About → Software Version. If you don't see "Add to Home Screen", you're not in Safari or iOS is too old.
